Cloak Watch reads new lines from local EVE GameLogs. It makes no network requests and never controls or modifies the EVE client.

It reports supported decloak messages only. It cannot determine whether a ship is continuously cloaked.

Cloak Watch is an unofficial player-made tool. Third-party tools are used at your own risk. It never automates gameplay; review CCP's Third Party Policies before use.
