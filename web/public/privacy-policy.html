LoW Manager Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LoW Manager and the LoW Manager Discord Bot collect, use, store, and delete information when you use the app or bot.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, LoW Manager may collect or process:

  • Account information, such as email address, login method, display pilot, and app role.
  • EVE Online pilot information authorized through EVE SSO, such as character IDs, names, portraits, skills, location, assets, wallet, fleet, industry, and other ESI data needed for enabled features.
  • Saved fits, doctrines, notes, Google Doc URLs, public/private library settings, and imported EFT fit text.
  • Discord server IDs, channel IDs, thread IDs, message IDs, author display names or IDs, timestamps, and message links needed to scan and import fits from Discord.
  • Discord message content only when an app user requests a channel or thread scan for fit import. The bot is intended to extract EFT-style fit blocks or visible pyfa screenshot content from recent messages.
  • Uploaded, pasted, or scanned images used for pyfa screenshot extraction.
  • Operational logs, audit/security events, error records, and usage metadata needed to run, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide LoW Manager features, including pilot dashboards, fits, doctrines, asset views, market estimates, contract search, Discord fit import, and in-game fit export.
  • Authenticate users and protect private account and pilot data.
  • Process user-requested Discord scans and convert matching fit content into saved fits.
  • Maintain public fit and doctrine libraries when users choose to publish them.
  • Monitor service health, debug issues, prevent abuse, and improve reliability.

3. Public and Private Data

Private account data, private pilot-derived data, and private fits or doctrines are intended to be visible only to the owning account and administrators. Fits and doctrines marked public may be visible to anyone with access to the app, including users who are not logged in.

4. Sharing and Service Providers

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers used to operate the app, such as hosting, database, email, authentication, Discord, EVE Online ESI, Google document access for user-provided public document URLs, and AI/OCR providers when pyfa screenshot extraction is used. Information may also be disclosed if required by law or to protect the service from abuse or security threats.

5. Data Retention

Data is kept only as long as needed to provide the service, maintain user-requested saved content, satisfy operational or security needs, or comply with legal obligations. Discord scan results are used for the requested import workflow; saved fits created from a scan remain until deleted by an authorized user or administrator. Operational logs and anonymized audit/security events may be retained for troubleshooting and security.

6. Deletion Requests

You may request deletion of your account-related private data, private pilot tokens, private pilot-derived data, private fits, or private doctrines by contacting cyrdax@gmail.com. Removing the bot from a Discord server stops future bot access to that server, but does not automatically delete previously imported fits or app records.

7. Security

LoW Manager uses access controls and reasonable technical safeguards to protect private data. No online service can guarantee perfect security, and users should avoid submitting sensitive information that is not needed for the app's features.

8. Children's Privacy

LoW Manager is not intended for children under 13 or anyone below the minimum age required to use Discord, EVE Online, or related third-party services in their region.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated as the app changes. The "Last updated" date will be revised when material changes are made.

10. Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to cyrdax@gmail.com.
